SEE IT. PICK IT. PLAY IT. KEEP MOVING.

The phone or cards handle the bookkeeping. The group gets the part that matters: fast choices, ridiculous attempts, guesses, and reactions.

4SCORE

PHYSICAL + DIGITAL

BUILT FOR THE TABLE. SMART ENOUGH FOR THE PHONE.

The physical game keeps the social energy in the room. The digital version can handle timing, scoring, drawing, and fast pass-and-play without becoming the center of attention.

PHYSICAL

A compact game-night box.

Cards, a reusable drawing surface, quick-start rules, and simple scoring. Add components only when playtesting proves they earn their place.

DIGITAL

Pass the phone. Keep the pace.

The prototype is offline-friendly, pass-and-play, local-first, and designed around the same choice—not a collection of separate mini-games.
