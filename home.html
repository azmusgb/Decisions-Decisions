THE DIFFERENCE

THE CHOICE IS THE GAME.

Most communication games tell you how to perform. GET THE POINT makes you choose first. The point values change by prompt, so the safest route is not always the smartest one.

THE PRODUCT PATH

CARDS FIRST. DIGITAL WHERE IT HELPS.

The goal is a polished physical party game with a credible iPhone path—not an overbuilt app wrapped around an unproven mechanic.

PHYSICAL

A compact game-night box.

Current prototype direction: roughly 100 double-sided cards, reusable drawing surface, marker, quick-start rules, simple scoring, and phone timing while the rules are validated.

See the roadmap →
DIGITAL

Pass the phone. Keep the pace.

The digital path is iPhone-first, offline, pass-and-play, one-tap commit, local state, minimal permissions, and content that can expand without changing the core mechanic.

See what we're testing →
NEXT MILESTONE

PROVE THE FUN.

The test is simple: can strangers understand the choice, laugh, finish a game, and want another one? That evidence comes before final rules, manufacturing, or a full native app.
