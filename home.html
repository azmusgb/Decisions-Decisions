WORKING COMMERCIAL TITLE · PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

ONE PROMPT. THREE WAYS TO PLAY.

GET THE POINT is a social party game about making a choice under pressure. Every prompt gives you multiple ways to communicate it, each with its own point value. Pick the route, commit, and get your team to guess before the clock runs out.

THE CORE IDEA

THE CHOICE IS THE GAME.

This is not Pictionary, charades, or karaoke bundled together. The differentiator is the decision before the performance.

FAST DECISIONS. FAST TURNS.

PRODUCT DIRECTION

PHYSICAL + DIGITAL.

The goal is a polished, manufacturable physical party game with a credible iPhone companion and standalone digital path.

Physical prototype

Current target is roughly 100 double-sided cards, a reusable drawing surface, marker, quick-start rules, scoring, and a phone timer during development.

iPhone-first

Offline, pass-and-play, one canonical game-state source, minimal permissions, one-tap commitment, and data-driven content.

Content engine

The mechanic stays stable while future packs expand the prompt library and personality rather than creating new mini-games.

CURRENT STATUS

BUILDING EVIDENCE BEFORE SCALE.

The primary milestone is simple: strangers understand the game, laugh, make meaningful choices, and want to play again.

What is locked

Choice under time pressure, prompt-specific risk/reward, irreversible commitment once clueing begins, and the requirement that correct guesses earn the selected points.

What is still being tested

HUM vs SOUND, skip/pass, Easy/Hard handling, timer start timing, final team structure, method labels/order/colors/icons, pricing, packaging, and the commercial name.
