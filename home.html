PHYSICAL + DIGITAL

SAME GAME. TWO FORMATS.

The physical and digital versions are built around the same choice. The format changes; the core mechanic does not.

PHYSICAL

A compact table game.

Cards, a reusable drawing surface, quick-start rules, and simple scoring. Components stay lean until playtesting proves they add value.
