BUILDING THE REAL PRODUCT

PHYSICAL + DIGITAL.

The target is a polished physical party game with a credible iPhone companion and standalone digital path—not a bloated app wrapped around an unproven mechanic.

PHYSICAL

Cards first.

Current target: roughly 100 double-sided cards, reusable drawing surface, marker, quick-start rules, simple scoring, phone timer during prototyping.

DIGITAL

iPhone-first.

Offline, pass-and-play, one-tap commit, persistent local state, minimal permissions, and data-driven content.

CONTENT

Packs expand the game.

New packs should add prompts and personality without fragmenting the core mechanic into separate mini-games.
