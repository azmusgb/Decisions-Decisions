PLAYING THE GAME

THE CORE LOOP

What is GET THE POINT?

A social party game built around choice under time pressure. Each prompt gives you multiple ways to communicate it with different prompt-specific point values. You pick one, start clueing, and your teammates try to guess before time runs out.

Is this just Pictionary, charades, and humming in one box?

No. Those are clueing tools. The game mechanic is the decision before the clue: one prompt, multiple ways to play, different risk/reward, and the player chooses which route to take.

How does scoring work?

Every prompt has its own point values by method. A method is not permanently worth more. If your team gets the answer, you earn the value attached to the method you picked for that prompt.

Can I switch after I start?

No. Once clueing begins, the chosen method stands. The digital prototype uses one tap to choose and commit so there is no extra confirmation step slowing the turn down.

What are the current methods?

The current challenger is HUM → DRAW → MIME using teal, yellow, and purple signals. These labels, order, colors, and the MIME icon remain PROPOSED / NEEDS PLAYTESTING.

Why HUM instead of SOUND?

HUM is the current challenger because it creates a tighter musical constraint. A broader SOUND method remains an active comparison condition.

PROTOTYPE + PLAYTESTING

WHAT IS STILL OPEN

What is the timer?

The current baseline is 60 seconds, then 30, then 15, plus a 15-second tiebreak condition. These are test defaults, not final commercial rules.

Can players pass?

Pass/skip behavior is unresolved. The current prototype compares one pre-choice skip against no skip. Ordinary post-choice passing is not part of the normal player flow in this test build.

Does the timer start when the prompt appears?

This is still being tested. The prototype can compare timer-start-on-reveal versus timer-start-on-choice behavior.

Why does the digital demo support swipe-to-score?

That is a PROPOSED / NEEDS PLAYTESTING interaction intended to make the phone feel more like a smart deck of cards. The visible Correct button remains the accessible fallback and canonical scoring action.

Does the browser demo use my camera or microphone?

No. The current private prototype does not require camera or microphone access. Game state and telemetry stay local on the device unless a tester explicitly exports data or submits feedback.

Can I play the current demo?

The current build is a private playtest tool. Invited testers can enter the private demo. Everyone else can use the public mechanic teaser or join the playtest pool.

What is the current success milestone?

Strangers understand the game, laugh, make meaningful choices, finish cleanly, and want to play again. That proof comes before heavy manufacturing spend or a full commercial app.

PHYSICAL + DIGITAL PRODUCT

WHAT WE ARE BUILDING TOWARD

What will the physical game include?

Current prototype direction is roughly 100 double-sided cards, a reusable drawing surface, dry-erase marker, quick-start rules, simple scoring, and a phone timer during development. Final materials, dimensions, dielines, and finishes require supplier validation.

Is there an app?

There is a working browser playtest prototype now. The preferred product direction is iPhone-first, offline, pass-and-play, with local content, minimal permissions, and no unnecessary backend.

Will there be subscriptions?

Not by default. The preferred model is a free or low-friction starter, a one-time Core unlock, optional one-time theme packs, and a separate physical edition.

How does new content expand the game?

Prompts are the expansion engine. New packs should create new choices and situations without inventing new core mechanics every time.

Will there be licensed packs?

Possibly later, but launch economics will not depend on major licensed brands. Rights safety remains a content gate.

NAME + LAUNCH

COMMERCIAL STATUS

Is GET THE POINT the final commercial name?

No. It is the current lead/name-to-beat. PICK YOUR POINT is the serious challenger, YOUR CALL is a wildcard, and POINT TAKEN is a control. Consumer preference and legal/trademark clearance are separate workstreams.

When will it launch?

There is no locked launch date. The project should not move to Kickstarter or preorder until blind-play evidence, naming work, manufacturing economics, fulfillment, schedule contingency, and a credible prototype are in place.

How can I follow the project?

Join the prelaunch list for meaningful milestones, or join a playtest if you want to help validate the game directly.
