FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE · SEPTEMBER 2026

GET THE POINT Introduces a New Party-Game Twist: Choose How You Play Before the Clock Wins

GET THE POINT, a new physical-and-digital social party game currently in development, is built around a deceptively simple idea: every prompt gives players multiple ways to communicate it, and the player chooses the risk before attempting the clue.

Rather than assigning one universal activity, GET THE POINT presents prompt-specific communication routes with different point values. The active player chooses a route, commits, performs, and teammates race to guess the prompt before the timer expires. Correct guesses earn the selected route's points.

The current prototype is testing a three-route challenger — HUM, DRAW, and MIME — along with a 60/30/15-second round structure. Those labels, timing rules, and visual details remain under active playtesting and should not be treated as final commercial specifications.

The project is being developed as both a compact physical game and an offline, pass-and-play digital experience. A working web prototype is already available for testing, while the physical prototype direction centers on a card deck, reusable drawing surface, quick-start rules, simple scoring, and a phone timer during development.

The development team is deliberately prioritizing blind playtesting before scaling manufacturing or software scope. The key product milestone is not feature completeness; it is whether new groups can understand the game in roughly two minutes, make meaningful route choices, laugh during play, and want another round.

GET THE POINT is also being developed with a content-driven expansion strategy. The core mechanic remains stable while future packs can introduce new prompt sets and visual personalities without fragmenting the game into separate mini-games.

GET THE POINT is currently a lead commercial-name candidate and remains subject to consumer testing and legal/trademark validation.
