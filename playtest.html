HOW THE TEST WORKS

NO COACHING. JUST PLAY.

The prototype should carry the experience. If the creator has to rescue a rule, explain a card, or steer a decision, that is useful evidence—not something to hide.

1OPEN

Use only the provided instructions.

2LEARN

See whether the group can start meaningful play quickly.

3PLAY

Finish a full game without strategy coaching.

4REACT

Tell us what worked, what dragged, and what you would change.

WHAT MATTERS

WHAT PEOPLE DO BEATS WHAT THEY SAY.

We care about the actual choices, timing, misses, rule questions, dead prompts, laughter, and whether the group wants to keep playing.

COMPREHENSION

Do people get it?

Can a new group understand the risk/reward idea and get into meaningful play in about two minutes?

CHOICE

Do points change behavior?

Do players switch between methods by prompt, or default to the same favorite every time?

ENERGY

Does it create a reaction?

We track funny failures, pace, dead cards, method dominance, rule questions, and replay interest.

STILL BEING TESTED

THE PROTOTYPE IS SUPPOSED TO MOVE.

Some details are intentionally unsettled so real play can still change them.

Methods

NEEDS PLAYTESTING: HUM vs SOUND, final labels/order/colors/icons, MIME comprehension, and the current method-control treatment.

Turn structure

NEEDS PLAYTESTING: timer start on reveal vs choice, skip/pass behavior, and the 60 → 30 → 15 second baseline.
