WHAT WE MEASURE

EVIDENCE, NOT POLISH.

We care about behavior more than compliments. The current build records local playtest telemetry and exposes unresolved settings rather than pretending they are final rules.

COMPREHENSION

Do people get it?

Can a new group explain the risk/reward choice back correctly and begin meaningful play in about two minutes?

CHOICE

Do points matter?

Do players change routes by prompt, or simply choose the same favorite method regardless of scoring?

ENERGY

Does it create a reaction?

We track funny failures, dead cards, route dominance, pace, rule questions, and whether the group wants another game.

CURRENT TEST VARIABLES

THESE ARE NOT FINAL RULES.

The current challenger exists to produce evidence. We will lock decisions only after the data supports them.

Route system

PROPOSED / NEEDS PLAYTESTING: HUM vs SOUND, HUM → DRAW → MIME order, teal/yellow/purple signals, and the theatrical-mask MIME icon challenger.

Turn structure

NEEDS PLAYTESTING: timer start on reveal vs commit, one pre-commit skip vs no skip, and the 60 → 30 → 15 second baseline.
