THE BLIND TEST

CAN THE PRODUCT CARRY THE EXPERIENCE?

The creator should not coach strategy, rescue weak prompts, or explain away confusing rules. If the product needs that help, the test found something valuable.

1OPEN

Use only the provided instructions.

2LEARN

Try to begin meaningful play quickly.

3PLAY

Finish a complete game without design coaching.

4REACT

Tell us where it worked—and where it broke.

WHAT WE MEASURE

BEHAVIOR BEFORE OPINION.

Compliments are nice. Choices, timing, failures, rule questions, and replay behavior are much more useful.

COMPREHENSION

Do people get it?

Can a new group explain the risk/reward idea back correctly and begin meaningful play in about two minutes?

CHOICE

Do points matter?

Do players change routes by prompt, or default to the same favorite method regardless of points?

ENERGY

Does it create a reaction?

We track funny failures, dead cards, route dominance, pace, rule questions, and whether the group wants another game.

CURRENT TEST VARIABLES

THESE ARE NOT FINAL RULES.

Route system
