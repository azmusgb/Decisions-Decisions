LAUNCH DISCIPLINE

PROOF BEFORE PROMISES.

A credible launch is the result of product evidence, supplier evidence, legal work, and realistic economics—not a date picked for marketing convenience.

MILESTONE-BASED

Launch when the game earns it.

Blind-play evidence, replay interest, strong content, and stable rules come first.

SUPPLIER-VALIDATED

Price the real product.

BOM, MOQ, freight, fulfillment, materials, and schedule need credible supplier inputs before launch promises.

USEFUL UPDATES

Signal, not noise.

Expect major prototype, testing, naming, manufacturing, and launch-readiness milestones—not a daily content treadmill.

THE GATE

WHAT MUST BE TRUE BEFORE CROWDFUNDING.

The project should reach Kickstarter or preorder only after the playable prototype survives blind testing, real footage proves the mechanic, manufacturing and fulfillment are credible, naming/trademark work is underway, and schedule contingency is built in.

Kickstarter's job

FINANCING + MARKET VALIDATION—not rescuing unfinished product design.
