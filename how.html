HOW IT WORKS

THE CHOICE IS THE GAME.

Every prompt gives the active player multiple communication routes with different point values. Decide fast, commit, perform, and get the team to the answer before the clock wins.

WHY IT FEELS DIFFERENT

THE SAME PROMPT CAN TEMPT YOU THREE WAYS.

The route values are prompt-specific. A route that looks easy on one card may be the risky play on another. That is where the strategy and comedy come from.
