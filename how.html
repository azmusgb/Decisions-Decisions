HOW IT WORKS

THE CHOICE IS THE GAME.

Every prompt gives the active player multiple communication routes with different point values. Decide fast, commit, perform, and get the team to guess before time runs out.

Reveal one prompt

The performer sees the target and route-specific point values.

Choose your route

Pick the route that balances confidence and points. In the current digital prototype, one tap selects and commits.

Perform under pressure

Hum, draw, or mime in the current challenger while teammates shout guesses.

Score and keep moving

Correct earns the selected points. Move to another prompt while time remains.
