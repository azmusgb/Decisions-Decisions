HOW IT WORKS

THE CHOICE IS THE GAME.

One prompt appears with three ways to play and three different point values. Pick one, start clueing, and get your team to the answer before the clock runs out.

1REVEAL

See one prompt and the three point values attached to it.

2PICK

Choose HUM, DRAW, or MIME based on confidence and reward.

3PLAY

Once clueing starts, that choice stands. Get your team guessing.

4SCORE

Correct earns the selected points. Move immediately to the next prompt.

TRY IT

THE POINT VALUES CHANGE THE DECISION.

The same way to play is not always worth the same amount. A comfortable choice can be worth one point while a riskier one is worth three. That is where the tension—and a lot of the comedy—comes from.

Pick a way

HUM for 3, DRAW for 1, or MIME for 2. What would you pick?

THE CLOCK

FAST TURNS. NO DEAD AIR.

The current timing baseline is 60 seconds, then 30, then 15. That progression—and whether the timer starts on reveal or on the first choice—still needs playtesting.

What is not under debate: turns should move fast, the choice should happen before performance, and the game should keep everyone involved.
