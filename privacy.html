PRIVACY

KEEP IT SIMPLE.

This project is in development. The public site collects only the information you choose to submit through contact, playtest, retail, manufacturing, or prelaunch forms.

Form information

Submitted names, email addresses, organizations, interests, and messages are used to respond to inquiries, organize playtesting, or maintain the requested interest list. Netlify Forms processes these submissions for the site.

Prototype data

The current playable prototype stores game state and playtest metrics locally on the device unless you explicitly export or share them. The prototype does not require an account.

No unnecessary permissions

The current web prototype does not require camera, microphone, or geolocation permissions.

Requests

If you want information you submitted corrected or removed from project records, use the contact page and identify the email address associated with the submission.
