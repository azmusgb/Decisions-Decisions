PRIVACY

KEEP IT SIMPLE.

This project is in development. The public site collects only information you choose to submit through its forms, while the current game prototype keeps ordinary game state and playtest metrics on the device unless you export or share them.

FORM INFORMATION

What you choose to send.

Names, email addresses, organizations, interests, and messages submitted through public forms are used to respond to inquiries, organize playtesting, or maintain the list you requested. Netlify Forms processes those submissions for the site.

PROTOTYPE DATA

Local by default.

The current playable prototype stores active game state and playtest telemetry locally on the device unless you explicitly export or share it. The prototype does not require an account.

PERMISSIONS

No camera or microphone required.

The current browser prototype does not need camera, microphone, or geolocation permissions. The site also sends a permissions policy that disables those capabilities on current public routes.

REQUESTS

Need something corrected?

If you want information you submitted corrected or removed from project records, use the contact page and identify the email address associated with the submission.
