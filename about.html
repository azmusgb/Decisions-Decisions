GET THE POINT
ABOUT THE GAME

THE DECISION HAPPENS BEFORE THE CLUE.

GET THE POINT is a social party game where every prompt gives you three ways to play. Each way is worth different points, so you choose how to go for the answer before you start clueing.

THE CORE IDEA

CHOICE UNDER TIME PRESSURE.

Most communication games tell you how to give the clue. GET THE POINT gives you a choice. See the prompt, compare the points, choose how to play it, then get your team to guess before time runs out.

One prompt. Three ways to play.

Different points make the choice matter every time.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

THE POINTS CHANGE WHAT YOU PICK.

The best choice is not always the easiest one.

CHOOSE

Play to your strengths.

Draw it, mime it, or try the musical route depending on the prompt and the points.

RISK

Chase the bigger score.

A harder option can be worth more, but only if your team can get there in time.

PACE

Keep moving.

Get it right, take the points, and move straight to the next prompt while time remains.

PHYSICAL + DIGITAL

SAME GAME. TWO FORMATS.

The format can change without changing what makes the game work.

PHYSICAL

Made for the table.

Prompt cards, a reusable drawing surface, quick-start rules, and simple scoring keep the attention on the people playing.

DIGITAL

Made to stay out of the way.

The digital direction is fast pass-and-play: reveal the prompt, choose, time the turn, score, and hand it off.

PLAYTESTING NOW

THE GAME HAS TO WORK WITHOUT US IN THE ROOM.

The next proof is simple: new groups understand it quickly, make meaningful choices, laugh, and want another round.
