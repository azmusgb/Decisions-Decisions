ABOUT THE GAME

THE DECISION HAPPENS BEFORE THE CLUE.

GET THE POINT is a social party game where one prompt can be played three different ways. Each way carries its own points, so the player has to choose between confidence and reward before the performance starts.

THE CORE IDEA

CHOICE UNDER TIME PRESSURE.

Most communication games tell you how to clue the answer. GET THE POINT gives you options. You see the prompt, compare the points, pick one way to play, and then live with that choice once clueing begins.

The mechanic

ONE PROMPT → MULTIPLE WAYS → DIFFERENT RISK/REWARD → PLAYER CHOOSES.

WHAT GOOD PLAY FEELS LIKE

FAST TO LEARN. FUN TO RISK.

The target is a game that explains quickly, keeps everyone involved, and turns both success and failure into a reaction worth remembering.

FAST START

About two minutes.

A new group should move from opening the game to meaningful play without a long rules lecture.

REAL CHOICE

Points should matter.

Players should weigh confidence against reward instead of choosing the same method every time.

GOOD FAILURE

Funny, not humiliating.

The best misses create stories, laughter, and instant rematches—not regret for taking the risk.

WHAT WE ARE BUILDING

ONE GAME. PHYSICAL AND DIGITAL.

The physical and digital versions should reinforce the same mechanic rather than split into separate mini-games or feature sets.

PHYSICAL

A compact table game.

Cards, a reusable drawing surface, quick-start rules, and simple scoring—kept lean until testing proves anything else deserves to be in the box.

DIGITAL

A smart deck in your pocket.

Offline-first, pass-and-play, fast choices, local state, drawing when needed, and as little screen friction as possible.

THE ROAD AHEAD

TEST FIRST. SCALE SECOND.

The game should earn expensive decisions through play evidence instead of locking them because they looked good in a prototype.

01

Prototype

Build a clean, comparable version and a strong candidate prompt pool.

02

Blind play

Test comprehension, method choice, pace, laughter, and replay interest.

03

Refine

Calibrate points, cut weak prompts, and resolve the rules and identity that survive testing.

04

Scale

Validate suppliers, economics, launch readiness, and the smallest native product worth shipping.

STILL OPEN

REAL PLAY CAN STILL CHANGE THESE.

The current prototype deliberately keeps several decisions open instead of pretending the commercial game is already finished.

Methods

HUM vs SOUND, final labels/order/colors/icons, and MIME comprehension remain NEEDS PLAYTESTING.

Rules

Skip/pass, timer start, 60/30/15 timing, Easy/Hard use, and final round/team structure remain NEEDS PLAYTESTING.

Commercial

Name clearance, pricing, package dimensions, materials, manufacturing, fulfillment, and launch timing require separate validation.

HELP SHAPE IT

PLAYTESTING IS THE NEXT FILTER.

The most useful feedback now is not whether an idea sounds good. It is what happens when a real group tries to play without coaching.
