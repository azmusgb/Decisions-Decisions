DEVELOPMENT STORY

PROVE THE FUN. THEN SCALE.

GET THE POINT is being developed as a real physical + digital party game. The team is deliberately validating the mechanic, content, naming, and economics before committing to expensive manufacturing or a full commercial app.

WHAT IS LOCKED

THE CORE IDEA.

The product differentiator is choice under time pressure: one prompt, multiple routes, prompt-specific points, irreversible commitment once clueing begins, and a team racing the clock.

Why it exists

Most communication games tell you how to perform. GET THE POINT makes choosing how to perform part of the game.

What success looks like

Strangers understand it quickly, make meaningful choices, laugh, and ask to play again.

WHAT IS STILL OPEN

TEST BEFORE LOCK.

Some visible details are intentionally provisional so playtest evidence can still change them.

Methods

HUM vs SOUND, final labels, order, icons, and signal colors remain playtest decisions.

Rules

Pass/skip behavior, Easy/Hard handling, timer-start timing, and final team/round structure remain open.

Commercial

Name clearance, pricing, packaging, manufacturing details, and launch timing require validation.

ROADMAP

PHYSICAL + DIGITAL PATH.

Prototype first, blind-play evidence next, then identity, iPhone vertical slice, manufacturing economics, and prelaunch demand.

01

Prototype

Freeze a testable rules condition and build candidate content.

02

Blind play

Validate comprehension, route choice, pacing, and replay interest.

03

Refine

Calibrate points, remove weak prompts, resolve naming and visual identity.

04

Scale carefully

Build the smallest iOS slice, validate manufacturing, then consider Kickstarter/preorder.
