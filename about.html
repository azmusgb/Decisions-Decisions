THE PROJECT

PROVE THE FUN. THEN SCALE.

GET THE POINT is being developed as a real physical + digital party game. The mechanic gets tested first. Expensive manufacturing, full native development, and launch promises come later.

WHAT IS LOCKED

CHOICE UNDER TIME PRESSURE.

One prompt gives the active player multiple ways to communicate it. Each route has prompt-specific points. The player chooses before performing, the choice locks once clueing begins, and teammates race the clock.

Core product truth

ONE PROMPT → MULTIPLE ROUTES → DIFFERENT RISK/REWARD → PLAYER CHOOSES.

WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

STRANGERS GET IT—AND WANT ANOTHER GAME.

The immediate goal is not feature count. It is comprehension, laughter, meaningful route choices, clean pacing, and replay interest.

FAST START

About two minutes.

A new group should move from opening the game to meaningful play without a long rules lecture.

REAL CHOICE

Points change behavior.

Players should compare confidence and reward rather than always choosing a favorite route.

GOOD FAILURE

Funny, not humiliating.

Misses should create stories and reactions without making the performer regret taking the risk.

ROADMAP

VALIDATE BEFORE YOU SCALE.

The path is deliberately staged so each expensive decision is downstream of evidence.

01

Prototype

Freeze a comparable test condition and build a strong candidate prompt pool.

02

Blind play

Test comprehension, route choice, pace, funny failure, and replay interest.

03

Refine

Calibrate points, cut weak prompts, resolve naming, rules, and visual identity.

04

Scale carefully

Validate suppliers and economics, then build the smallest native slice worth having.

STILL OPEN

TEST BEFORE LOCK.

Visible prototype details are intentionally provisional where real play can still change them.

Methods

HUM vs SOUND, final labels, order, icons, signal colors, and control sizing remain NEEDS PLAYTESTING.

Rules

Skip/pass, timer start, 60/30/15 timing, Easy/Hard use, and final round/team structure remain NEEDS PLAYTESTING.

Commercial

Name clearance, pricing, package dimensions, materials, manufacturing, fulfillment, and launch timing require separate validation.
